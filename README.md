## 🛡️ 2. The "Don't Get Sued" Operational Checklist

Follow this 5-point safety rule on **every single client project**:

1. **Always Send the Contract Before Starting**:
   - Open `contracts/web_design_agreement.md`.
   - Fill in your name, the client's name, the project price, and the governing state/country.
   - Have the client sign (digitally via DocuSign, HelloSign, or email confirmation).
2. **Collect 50% Upfront**:
   - Never write code or buy domains before the initial deposit clears into your account.
3. **Enforce the 2-Round Revision Cap**:
   - Require clients to send feedback in one consolidated list. Any changes after 2 rounds are billed hourly ($50–$100/hr).
4. **Client Owns Hosting & Domain**:
   - Have the client create and pay for their own GoDaddy/Namecheap/Vercel account so you are not liable for recurring hosting bills or domain renewal lapses.
5. **Transfer Ownership Only on Full Payment**:
   - Your contract explicitly states that the client only owns the design/code once the final 50% invoice is paid.
