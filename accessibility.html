Accessibility Statement

Vanguard Studio is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We continuously improve the user experience for everyone and apply relevant accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1 AA level compliance).

Standards & Engineering Safeguards:

  • Semantic HTML5 structure and accessible skip-to-main links
  • Full keyboard navigation support and visible focus indicators
  • ARIA landmarks, dialog focus traps, and screen-reader status announcements
  • Strict adherence to OS prefers-reduced-motion settings

If you experience any accessibility barriers, please contact us at vanguardstudio.sample@gmail.com so we can assist promptly.
