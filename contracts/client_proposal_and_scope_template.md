## 4. Intellectual Property & Account Ownership Summary
All intellectual property is governed by the **Four-Bucket IP Model** detailed in the Master Web Design Agreement:
1. **Client Materials**: Client retains 100% ownership of pre-existing logos, photos, copy, and customer records.
2. **Custom Deliverables**: Bespoke layouts and written copy transfer to the Client **upon receipt of 100% cleared final payment**.
3. **Vanguard Background IP**: Reusable design systems, UI components, and JavaScript motion governors remain studio property; Client receives a perpetual, royalty-free operating license (transferable upon sale of the business or website asset, non-extractable as standalone tools).
4. **Third-Party Materials**: Open-source libraries (Tailwind, Google Fonts) remain governed by their respective licenses (MIT, SIL).

**Domain & Account Ownership**: The Client maintains primary ownership and control of its domain names and core accounts. Hosting administration and ongoing technical support may be provided by Vanguard under an optional managed Care Plan retainer.
