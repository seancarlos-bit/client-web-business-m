## 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ARCHITECTURE (FOUR-BUCKET MODEL)

The Parties explicitly establish a four-bucket intellectual property framework to govern all assets involved in this project:

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                     INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY FRAMEWORK                       │
├─────────────────────┬──────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Client Materials │ 2. Custom Deliverables│ 3. Designer Background IP    │
│ (Logos, Copy, Data) │ (Custom Site Layout) │ (Design Systems, JS Engines) │
│ ──► Client Retains  │ ──► Transferred Upon │ ──► Designer Retains;        │
│     Full Ownership  │     Full Payment     │     Perpetual Operating      │

│ ──► Governed by Original Open-Source & Vendor Licenses                    │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Bucket 1 — Client Materials (Pre-Existing Client IP)**:
   - The Client retains sole and exclusive ownership of all trademarks, trade names, logos, supplied photography, text copy, business records, and customer data provided to the Designer ("**Client Materials**").
   - The Client grants the Designer a limited, non-exclusive, revocable, royalty-free license to host, copy, adapt, and use Client Materials solely as required to perform services under this Agreement.
2. **Bucket 2 — Custom Deliverables (Project-Specific Work)**:
   - Conditioned strictly upon **receipt of 100% full and cleared payment of all project invoices**, the Designer assigns and transfers to the Client all worldwide right, title, and interest (including copyright) in and to the custom visual designs, custom written text authored by Designer specifically for Client, and project-specific HTML/CSS layout created uniquely for this project ("**Custom Deliverables**").
   - **No transfer of ownership occurs prior to cleared receipt of the final balance.** If this Agreement terminates prior to final payment, no title or copyright in Custom Deliverables transfers to the Client.
3. **Bucket 3 — Designer Background IP (Reusable Tools & Frameworks)**:
   - The Designer retains sole and exclusive ownership of all pre-existing tools, proprietary frameworks, design token specifications (`DESIGN.md`), reusable UI primitives, motion governors and JavaScript interaction engines (`js/motion.js`), starter templates, workflows, algorithms, and general know-how developed prior to or independently of this project ("**Designer Background IP**").
   - Conditioned upon full payment of all amounts due under this Agreement, the Designer grants the Client a **perpetual, irrevocable, non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license** to display, execute, and operate the Designer Background IP solely as integrated within the delivered website.
   - **Permitted Business Transfer**: This operating license may be assigned or transferred by the Client without the Designer's prior written consent solely in connection with the bona fide sale, merger, corporate acquisition, reorganization, or transfer of substantially all of the Client's business or assets, or the transfer of the specific website asset, provided that the assignee or transferee agrees in writing to be bound by all license terms and restrictions of this Agreement.
   - **Restrictions**: Except for the permitted business transfer above, the Client shall not sublicense, assign, sell, rent, lease, extract, decompile, reverse engineer, or distribute Designer Background IP as standalone software, component libraries, templates, or reusable design systems, nor use it to create derivative development tools for third parties. `[COUNSEL REVIEW RECOMMENDED: Ensure license scope and transfer mechanics align with applicable copyright transfer requirements.]`
4. **Bucket 4 — Third-Party Materials**:
   - Software libraries (e.g., Tailwind CSS), web fonts (e.g., Google Fonts), stock photography, icons, form endpoints, and API services remain governed exclusively by their respective third-party open-source or commercial licenses (e.g., MIT, Apache 2.0, SIL Open Font License).

## 10. TERMINATION & PROJECT CLOSURE

1. **Termination for Cause**: Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to cure such breach within **seven (7) business days** of receiving written notice specifying the breach.
2. **Termination for Convenience**: The Client may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time upon **seven (7) business days** prior written notice.
3. **Financial Settlement Upon Termination**:
   - In the event of termination by the Client for convenience or by the Designer for cause:
     - The Designer retains the initial Mobilization & Capacity Reservation Fee (50%) to cover onboarding, discovery, and calendar time committed;
     - The Client shall pay for any approved Change Orders and additional milestone work completed up to the date of termination; and
     - In no event shall previously paid milestone fees be subject to refund for completed work phases, subject to applicable law.
4. **Disposition of Deliverables & Materials**: Upon receipt of all outstanding payments due at termination, the Designer will deliver all completed and in-progress custom files in their current state ("as-is"). If termination occurs prior to full final payment, no license or ownership in Custom Deliverables or Designer Background IP is transferred. The Designer will return all Client Materials upon request.
