## Phase 1: Foundation, Entity & Legal Setup (Days 1–3)

### Goal: Establish clean operational boundaries, asset sourcing, and legal templates before contacting prospects.

1. **Select 1 High-Ticket Service Niche**:
   - Target businesses with substantial transaction values where a single new client covers the website investment:
     - **Home Services**: Roofers, HVAC specialists, licensed electricians, plumbers, kitchen/bath remodelers.
     - **Health & Clinical**: Cosmetic dentists, orthodontists, specialized physical therapy, med-spas.
     - **Commercial Services**: Commercial cleaning, business insurance brokers, local logistics.
   - *Unit Economics*: A roofing contractor earning $12,000–$18,000 per project readily invests $1,500–$2,500 in a digital flagship if it secures just one incremental commercial lead.

2. **Establish the Legal & Contractual Pipeline**:
   - Master Agreement: [`contracts/web_design_agreement.md`](file:///c:/Projects/client-web-business/contracts/web_design_agreement.md).
   - Proposal & Scope Sheet: [`contracts/client_proposal_and_scope_template.md`](file:///c:/Projects/client-web-business/contracts/client_proposal_and_scope_template.md).
   - Administrative Gate: Run through the **Pre-Signature Validation Checklist** (Section 12 of the Master Agreement) to resolve all brackets before issuing documents.
   - Business Account: Maintain dedicated business banking (e.g., Mercury, Relay, or separate business checking) to keep client funds distinct from personal capital.

3. **Zero-Risk Commercial Asset Sourcing**:
   - **Photography**: High-resolution client job-site photos first; supplement with commercially licensed photography from Unsplash or Pexels.
   - **Typography**: Google Fonts (open SIL Open Font License, zero commercial licensing fees).
   - **Icons**: Lucide Icons or Heroicons (MIT license).
   - **Code Primitives**: Permissive open-source libraries only (MIT / Apache 2.0).

## Phase 3: Client Prospecting & Targeted Outreach (Days 8–14)

### Goal: Secure 3–5 qualified discovery calls with established local business operators.

### The Google Maps Pipeline Method
1. Search local business categories: `"[Niche] in [City, State]"` (e.g., `"HVAC Contractors in Charlotte, NC"`).
2. Skip paid ad carousels and examine listings on **Pages 2 through 5**.
3. Identify candidates meeting any of these criteria:
   - Missing website URL on Google Business Profile.
   - Non-responsive desktop-only site failing on mobile viewports.
   - Unencrypted connection (`http://` instead of `https://`).
   - Outdated copyright year or broken inquiry forms.
4. Document Owner Name, Direct Email, and Phone Number from public business registries or state contractor databases.

### Concise Value-First Outreach Script
Send this personalized message via email or direct outreach:

> **Subject**: Mobile question regarding [Business Name]'s website
>
> *"Hi [Owner Name],*
>
> *I was reviewing top-rated [HVAC / roofing / plumbing] providers in [City] and came across [Business Name].*
>
> *I noticed that your site currently requires manual pinch-to-zoom on smartphones, which makes it harder for homeowners to tap and call you directly during emergency repairs.*
>
> *I built a lightweight, mobile-first prototype specifically tailored for local trade contractors: [Link to Live Demo].*
>
> *Would you be open to a 10-minute call this Thursday to see how this could improve your direct phone inquiries?*
>
> *Best regards,*  
> *[Your Name]*  
> *[Your Phone Number]"*

## Phase 4: Discovery, Value Pricing & Capacity Reservation (Days 15–18)

### Goal: Qualify the client, establish scope boundaries, sign the agreement, and secure the 50% mobilization fee.

### 15-Minute Discovery Framework
1. **Uncover Commercial Pain**: *"Where do your best customer inquiries originate today? What is your typical project ticket size?"*
2. **Demonstrate Speed & Craft**: Walk through the demo on a mobile viewport; show instant click-to-dial and sub-second load times.
3. **Present Defined Packages**:
   - **Starter Commercial Flagship**: **$1,200 – $1,500** (Single high-converting page with 5 structured sections, mobile optimization, technical SEO baseline, form routing).
   - **Multi-Section Authority Flagship**: **$1,800 – $2,500** (Expanded service breakdowns, case study galleries, interactive quote estimator, customized local schema).
   - **Turnkey Integration Package**: **$2,500 – $3,500** (Full multi-page architecture, CRM/scheduling ingestion, advanced local review integration).

## Phase 5: Production, Preview & Managed Revisions (Days 19–25)

### Goal: Deliver production deliverables within 14–21 days while preventing scope creep.

1. **Structured Content Intake (Days 19–20)**:
   - Provide a shared asset folder for the client to upload logos, high-res photos, and company credentials.
   - Enforce Section 3.1: Client must furnish materials within 7 business days to maintain production scheduling.
2. **Staging Assembly (Days 21–23)**:
   - Deploy code to a private edge staging URL (e.g., `clientname.preview.studio.com`).
3. **Consolidated Revision Round 1 (Day 24)**:
   - Provide client access to the staging environment.
   - Request **one single, consolidated punch-list** detailing all copy and styling adjustments. Fragmented, daily requests are excluded.
4. **Minor Polish & Round 2 (Day 25)**:
   - Apply consolidated tweaks.
   - Run Round 2 for typographical fixes and minor adjustments.
   - Request written approval or invoke the 7-day Review Period (Section 4.4).
5. **Handling Out-of-Scope Requests**:
   - If the client requests additional sections, custom booking engines, or structural redesigns, issue a formal **Change Order** billed at the standard rate (**$[HOURLY RATE]/hour**, recommended $100–$150/hr).

### Goal: Build high-margin, operationally capped recurring revenue while eliminating unmanaged support churn.

Avoid the low-margin trap of bundling open-ended marketing or manual edits into a cheap maintenance plan. Vanguard operates on a disciplined **$149 / $299 / $499+ ladder** anchored by strict labor ceilings and defined service boundaries:

### Detailed Tier Architecture & SLA Design

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **SLA Architecture Principle**:  
> **Response SLA $\ne$ Resolution SLA.** Vanguard contracts for rapid human response, triage, and deployment of fixes within its control. Vanguard does **not** promise fixed resolution times dependent on third-party infrastructure (e.g., DNS registrar delays, Cloudflare edge outages, or client email server failures).

1. **Tier 1: Care ($149/month)**:
   - **Included Services**: Managed edge hosting configuration, automated SSL certificate renewal, 24/7 automated synthetic uptime and SSL monitoring, monthly live form routing test, DNS record maintenance.
   - **Internal Labor Ceiling**: Target average $\le 20$ minutes/month; hard cap $\le 30$ minutes/month.
   - **SLA Commitment**: Automated 24/7 incident alerting; next-business-day human response for support requests; business-hours critical acknowledgement.
   - **Strict Boundary**: Zero included hours for custom design, copywriting, or marketing.

2. **Tier 2: Growth ($299/month)**:
   - **Included Services**: Everything in Tier 1 plus up to **1 hour per calendar month** of consolidated content/media updates (e.g., updating price lists, staff photos, operating hours, seasonal promotions).
   - **Performance**: Quarterly Core Web Vitals audit and cache optimization.
   - **Internal Labor Ceiling**: Target average $\le 45$ minutes/month; hard cap $\le 60$ minutes/month.
   - **SLA Commitment**: Priority business-hours human response (within 24 business hours).
   - **Rule**: Content hours are non-cumulative and expire at month-end.

3. **Tier 3: Growth+ ($499+/month)**:
   - **Included Services**: Full website infrastructure management paired with an explicit, quantified monthly local growth allowance:
     - Up to 2 scheduled Google Business Profile (GBP) posts or photo additions per month.
     - 1 review capture workflow or customer intake form optimization per month.
     - 1 landing page or service section conversion/on-page enhancement per month.
     - 1 monthly search visibility and inbound inquiry analytics digest.
   - **Internal Labor Ceiling**: Target average $\le 90$ minutes/month; hard cap $\le 120$ minutes (2.0 hours) total monthly implementation.
   - **SLA Commitment**: Expedited business-hours response; 4-business-hour critical incident acknowledgement.
   - **Strict Rule**: Any work exceeding the 2-hour monthly allowance requires a formal Change Order at **$[HOURLY RATE]/hour**.

### Labor Capacity Analysis & Organizational Inflection Points

1. **10 Clients (5.75 hrs/mo expected $\approx$ 1.33 hrs/week; 8.0 hrs max $\approx$ 1.85 hrs/week)**:
   - *Capacity Assessment*: Frictionless. Handled effortlessly alongside primary client builds with zero calendar conflict.
2. **25 Clients (14.75 hrs/mo expected $\approx$ 3.40 hrs/week; 20.5 hrs max $\approx$ 4.73 hrs/week)**:
   - *Capacity Assessment*: Sweet-spot solo agency model. Yields **~$5,825 MRR (~$70k ARR)** at under 4 hours per week of typical support and monitoring.
3. **50 Clients (28.75 hrs/mo expected $\approx$ 6.63 hrs/week; 40.0 hrs max $\approx$ 9.23 hrs/week)**:
   - *Inflection Point 1 (Automation & Batching)*: Support requests begin colliding with daytime client discovery calls.
   - *Required Action*: Centralize all requests through structured ticketing; batch routine content changes into a designated weekly operational block.
4. **100 Clients (57.50 hrs/mo expected $\approx$ 13.27 hrs/week; 80.0 hrs max $\approx$ 18.46 hrs/week)**:
   - *Inflection Point 2 (Delegation)*: Support volume approaches a half-time commitment (~13–18.5 hrs/week).
   - *Contractor Delegation Model*: If the founder delegates the 57.5 expected monthly support hours to a qualified technical contractor at **$30.00/hour**, the imputed internal labor ($3,450) is **replaced** (not double-counted) by contractor wages ($1,725). Modeled contribution before overhead, taxes, and owner compensation becomes:
     $$\$22,900\text{ (MRR)} - \$1,000\text{ (Infra)} - \$1,725\text{ (Contractor)} = \mathbf{\$20,175/\text{month}}$$
     Under the 100% contractual cap allowance (80.0 hrs @ $30/hr = $2,400), contribution before overhead remains **$19,500/month**. This provides ample cushion for operating overhead while fully freeing founder time for strategic sales.

## 🛡️ Risk Allocation & Contract Alignment Matrix

This matrix maps common agency risks to the corresponding protective mechanisms in the Master Web Design Agreement:

| Operational Risk | Potential Impact | Contract Provision & Protective Mechanism |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Scope Creep & Endless Revisions** | Client requests dozens of design tweaks or new feature additions without budget. | **Section 1.3 & Section 4**: Revisions are capped at two (2) consolidated rounds limited to styling/text. Out-of-scope additions require written Change Orders at **$[HOURLY RATE]/hour**. |
| **Client Delay & Mid-Project Inactivity** | Client stops responding for months, locking up production calendar time. | **Section 3.3 & Section 3.4**: Delays extend timeline day-for-day. 30 days of inactivity triggers formal notice; 14-day failure to cure results in Abandonment with fees retained. Reactivation requires a **[15%] fee**. |
| **Unapproved Design Re-Use** | Client cancels before final payment and launches the staging code independently. | **Section 5.2**: Custom Deliverables transfer copyright **only upon 100% cleared final payment**. No title or license transfers prior to full settlement. |
| **Reusable Tools & Design Systems** | Client claims exclusive ownership of agency UI libraries or motion governors. | **Section 5.3**: Studio retains sole ownership of Background IP (`DESIGN.md`, `js/motion.js`); Client receives a perpetual operating license for the site, transferable upon business sale but non-extractable. |
